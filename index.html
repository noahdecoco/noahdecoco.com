Creative coder & musician

Born and raised in Goa, India. Based in Amsterdam since 2015.

I build things for the browser that you can play with: shaders, particles and sound. Everything on this TV is drawn and generated live in code.

I also make immersive live music with instruments collected from my travels, plus my voice, hands and body. In 2024 I founded Safe and Sound Journeys.
